Who he was to us

To the world a barrister. To us, Uncle.

His names carry a whole way of living. We kept coming back to them while we wrote this page.

Abdul Razaq

عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ

Servant of Ar-Razzaq, the Provider. One of the most beautiful names of Allah, the One who gives sustenance to everything He created.

Olufemi

Olúfẹ́mi

Yoruba for “God loves me.” The name he carried from the family he came from, a reminder to trust in a love that does not leave.

We cannot give him anything now except what the Prophet ﷺ taught us still reaches him.

Grief wants somewhere to go. We built this page so that ours could go into prayer. A man of the law spent his life weighing what was just and what was merciful. Today we ask the One who is most Just and most Merciful to weigh him gently.

Read a prayer. Say ameen. Count a dhikr in his name. Then send this page to someone in the family, so that all of us who loved him are praying for him together.

Ongoing charity

Give something in his name and let it keep giving: a meal, a school fee, a well, a share in a mosque. Any good that continues counts.

Knowledge that helps

Pass on what you learned from him. A lesson told to a younger cousin is knowledge that is still being benefited from.

A child who prays

Those he loved are the ones who can pray for him. Teach the children of this family his name, and teach them to say it in du'a.
